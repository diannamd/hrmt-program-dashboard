The Diploma's Future

Scenario A: Revitalize

Modernize the diploma to compete directly.

+Competitors projected to shrink (international enrollment tightening)
+Domestic demand for HR credentials remains
?True domestic market size unknown
-Requires significant investment (online delivery, marketing)
-AI automating the entry-level roles this diploma prepares for

Scenario B: Evolve

Reposition as a specialized credential or degree bridge.

+BComm HR degree growing steadily (146 to 215, +47%)
+HRMT 100 core creates natural pipeline to degree
+Aligns with employer demand for strategic HR skills
-Loses diploma-seeking students to competitors
-Fewer total HR students in the near term
CPHR data: 83 student members (60 degree, 23 diploma). Only 3 wrote the NKE in 2024-25, likely because qualifying GPAs can waive it. Of those who wrote: degree holders passed (2/2), diploma did not (0/1). Takeaway: our accreditation is the value, and the degree pathway produces stronger outcomes.
AQA implications: 6 of 8 AQA recommendations (M1, M2, M3, M4, A2, A4) depend on this direction. Two are already underway (A1: first-year instruction, A3: BWIL 200 elective). Use the Action Worksheet to confirm the sequencing.
